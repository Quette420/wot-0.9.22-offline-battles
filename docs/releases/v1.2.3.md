# v1.2.3 - Bot combat and base defense

## Bot engagement, hull turning, and firing

- **Engagement takes priority over routine travel.** Bots changing routes, driving to a waiting position, or returning to a capture circle after being pushed out can suspend that travel task and engage once the engagement conditions are met. They resume the original task when the engagement ends.
- **Bots can turn first when they need to align the hull.** Removed the full-turn preview for all vehicles, the additional Siege Mode review, and the heading locks and waits imposed by those checks. Collision and ground support are handled during the actual turn. Before firing the first round of each automatic trigger pull, direct-fire Bots check the current muzzle alignment, shell readiness, and the actual shot path for obstructions.
- **Bots stay engaged while rechecking a shot.** Removed the two one-second disengagement timers for ordinary vehicles waiting to recheck a firing opportunity or recheck a blocked aim point while turning. Removed the six-second disengagement timers for pending HE aim-point and hull-turn checks. After reaching a new firing position, Bots recheck the aim point from their actual position and orientation.

## Waiting positions and Siege Mode

- **Vehicles without Siege Mode can turn their hulls toward the current enemy at waiting positions.** This applies to vehicles with fully traversable turrets, limited-traverse turrets, and fixed guns. The waiting task holds forward and reverse movement while allowing the vehicle to turn within its steering limits.
- **Siege-capable vehicles enter Siege Mode before finding the firing point.** A clear line to the same enemy with the same shell for **one second** confirms entry into combat. The candidate point may change on that enemy during confirmation, and it is used only to confirm entry into combat. Once Siege Mode is active, the Bot makes a fresh aim-point selection. The hull can turn and move short distances in Siege Mode, and aiming progress follows the actual movement once the hydraulics take over.
- **Siege engagements track loss of the firing line to the current enemy.** Once Siege Mode is active, a confirmed obstruction or loss of spotting starts the timer. A clear line resets it. When the firing line has been unavailable for **more than six seconds**, the Bot ends the engagement and leaves Siege Mode.
- **Waiting and capture tasks can hold Siege Mode.** A Siege-capable Bot enters Siege Mode while executing a wait at its waiting position, or after it has stopped inside the capture circle and is carrying out a capture order. It stays in Siege Mode for the duration of the task, even if the enemy leaves.

## Base defense

- **Travel takes priority before entering the circle, and engagement takes priority inside it.** A Bot returning to defend its base keeps its hull on the return route while the gun tracks the enemy and fires when the shot conditions are met. Once inside, it engages whenever it can.
- **Defenders search the circle from four directions when they cannot engage.** The search points are generally halfway from the center to the edge. Individual points are adjusted for the terrain, and multiple defenders spread out where possible. Reaching a point counts as a visit. After **six seconds** without progress toward it, the Bot tries another point. If the base is still being captured after all four points have been attempted, the Bot starts another pass and retries points that were blocked in the previous pass. Search pauses during an engagement.

- While automatic driving has control, **W, A, S, D, R, and F** are intercepted as driving inputs. In battle, press the **backtick key** (`` ` ``) immediately to the left of **1 on the main keyboard** to return to manual control and restore those six keys.
