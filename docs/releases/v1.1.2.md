# v1.1.2 — Combat, earnings and garage fixes

Built from `v1.1.1` on the `release/v1.1.x` maintenance branch.

## Garage and crew

- Customization: buying and applying a camouflage, paint, decal or effect in one step works again. #1513 sends this as a single buy-and-equip outfit command; the garage now buys only the missing units, charges for them and installs the outfit as one transaction instead of refusing with "not enough customization units". If the save cannot be written, the purchase is rolled back.
- Crew retraining: the three schools lose role level as in retail. Same vehicle class: free course 80%, credit school 90%, gold academy 100%. Another class: 60%, 80% and 100%. The school minimum (50% / 75% / 100%) still applies. Sandbox saves keep the same rules at no cost.
- Crew members still injured at the end of a battle earn 10% less crew XP (WG support article 15085).
- New saves and a factory reset use updated default equipment priorities: heavy tanks lead with a spall liner, tank destroyers and SPGs take binoculars. Existing saves keep their equipment.

## Battle mechanics

- Fire damage: a fire now starts at its strongest and weakens linearly until it burns out, instead of a fixed 5% of maximum HP every second for ten seconds. Untrained crews burn for ten seconds and lose about 25% of maximum HP. Each fire draws its starting strength once (80% to 120%), so a stronger fire also lasts longer. Firefighting and other crew effects from the client shorten the fire; they do not reduce its starting damage. Bots and players use the same model. The shape comes from community measurements; the ten-second duration and 5% starting rate are this project's own calibration, not retail data.
- Repairs: the toolbox now speeds up every module repair for players, and an injured crew member slows a player's repair as it already did for Bots. Repair time also scales with the number of conscious crew members (offline calibration).
- Spall liners now reduce the chance that a hit injures the crew.
- Recon with destroyed optics uses the client's own destroyed-device coefficient for players and Bots.
- Enhanced suspension reduces hull damage from landings that hit the tracks.
- Shot dispersion applies the 9.6 central-zone correction (inner zone about 10% instead of 16%); the exact sampling inside the zone is reconstructed.
- Rams: armour absorbs ram damage at full strength while the quarter-energy coefficient is kept, so rams against armoured hulls deal much less damage. This is an offline calibration, not a native constant.

## Earnings

- Battle results use a new economy model. Credits follow the 2015 community credit model with per-vehicle coefficients; XP follows the published WG categories with offline coefficients and tier weighting. Overlapping track, spotting and stun assistance is paid once. Chinese special base-XP vehicles, a +5% premium tier IX/X settlement factor and the Battle Hero/Epic resistance bonus are included, and mastery thresholds follow the regional base-XP factors.

## Bots

- Elite Bots carry premium rounds in place of silver penetration rounds and premium HE in place of silver HE; HE-primary guns and the SU-100Y / ISU-130 keep silver AP backups. Bot device priorities follow the new defaults, SU-5 carries only OF420SO, and M3G FT, Pz38t, Stuart I and Chi-He use their full guns.
- Bots no longer brake and turn back toward a narrow passage they have already crossed, drop stale bypasses of occupied route points, and join the outgoing route leg after bypassing a wreck at a route gate.
- A late worker callback catches up within a bounded time budget instead of replanning on every physics slice.

## Stability and interface

- Fixed a crash on game exit: model and engine-sound owners are now released before the client shuts down its model manager, and team chat no longer notifies a chat service that has already closed.
- Fixed an assertion when an ammo-rack explosion supersedes a destroyed-model request.
- Cancelling the pre-battle warning (for example, no ammunition) no longer reopens it forever.
- The ten-battle award shows the ordinary milestone text instead of the PvE text.
- Closing the simulation worker after a finished battle is no longer reported as a worker failure.
- Launcher: vehicle-class names are consistent across editors, and "show error report" selects the real file.
- Align source and executable versions to 1.1.2.
