# v1.2.2 - Bilingual UI and combat updates

## Bug fixes and localization

- The free-use notice now appears in Chinese and English in the same dialog on first launch and when the launcher is reopened after a factory reset.
- Prompts and activity log messages for saves, vehicle editing, Bot tactics, lineups, error reports, repairs, and resets follow the launcher’s language setting.
- The LAN lobby’s server address and messages for joining, disconnecting, reconnecting, and failing to start a battle use the selected language. The launcher recognizes server address prefixes in both languages.
- Messages for missing replays, failed validation, incompatible versions, and interrupted playback are available in both languages, along with errors for vehicle purchases with bonds and Personal Reserve transactions.
- Player automatic control messages and control panel details follow the selected language. This includes component and crew names, aiming status, firing checks, and command feedback.
- Mod-provided shell tooltips are available in both languages. They include shell velocity in the Garage and shell type, damage, penetration, blast radius, and range information in battle. Messages for ammo rack explosions caused by fire or falls also follow the selected language.

## Combat engagement

- Vehicles without Siege Mode can engage immediately once they have a valid firing opportunity. Siege-capable vehicles use a one-second confirmation before taking over from route driving.
- After a failed Siege Mode engagement ends, the vehicle retries the same target when the firing lane or aiming conditions improve. Selecting another aim point alone no longer causes repeated Siege Mode transitions.

## Battle mechanics and equipment

- **Fire damage** decreases linearly as the fire burns, for both player vehicles and Bots. The initial damage rate is set once per fire, between 80% and 120% of the base rate. Hull damage is applied from the accumulated total so that rounding on individual frames does not discard damage.
- **The crew’s Firefighting skill and its directive** make fire damage fall off faster, putting the fire out sooner and reducing total damage. They affect the decay after ignition. If a crew member is injured during a fire, the fire keeps its current progress and continues using the crew’s effective Firefighting ability.
- **Track and module repair speed** accounts for crew members still able to work, along with bonuses from the Repairs skill, Toolbox, directives, and consumables. Incapacitated crew members no longer contribute to repair speed.
- **Equipment and consumables** take effect according to the vehicle’s fitted loadout. Spall Liners affect crew injury checks from direct hits and explosions. Improved Suspension reduces hull damage when the tracks absorb a landing impact. Bot rations and fuel use the configured loadout, and engine wear with Removed Speed Governor active accrues based on time spent operating at low speed.
- **Base capture and artillery positioning** use actual vehicle positions. Bots can stop inside the capture circle while continuing to aim and fire, and try to re-enter if pushed out. Automatically controlled artillery and Bot artillery share their team’s position reservations so they can avoid taking the same firing position.

## Garage, saves, and battle results

- **Default equipment** is selected by vehicle class and compatibility. Light tanks prioritize Coated Optics, heavy tanks prioritize Spall Liners, and medium tanks, tank destroyers, and artillery prioritize Gun Rammers.
- **Upgrading older saves** rebuilds default and fully unlocked saves that still use the old equipment configuration. This one-time rebuild clears those saves’ old Garage data, battle history, account state, and associated backups.
- **Crew experience** uses each crew member’s condition at the end of the battle. Crew members who are still injured earn 10% less crew XP. Crew members healed before the battle ends earn the normal amount.
- **Credit earnings** distinguish damage dealt to targets you spot yourself or hit blind, damage dealt to targets spotted by teammates, and assisted damage. The rewards use vehicle-specific credit coefficients based on 375 historical test records, with estimates from comparable vehicles where records are unavailable.
- **Battle XP** is based on actual battle events. Eligible players on the losing team can receive the Courageous Resistance bonus. Double base XP for the gold versions of the Type 59, Type 62, and WZ111T counts toward battle standings and mission statistics.
- **Garage purchases and crew retraining** have been corrected. Buying and applying camouflage uses available inventory first and buys only the missing quantity. Crew retraining applies the appropriate proficiency loss when moving within or between vehicle classes.

## Test scenes and stability

- Equipment comparison scenes use the same vehicles, modules, and base crew qualifications so equipment, skills, and directives can be compared under the same conditions. Artillery also obeys the stationary order in the stationary-target earnings test.
- Fixed a delayed wreck model refresh that could restore the appearance of a turret after an ammo rack explosion had blown it off.
- Corrected the cleanup order for vehicle models, engine sounds, and connections when leaving a battle. A normal shutdown of the background simulation no longer produces a disconnect warning.

## How to use the Testing tab

All six checkboxes on the Testing tab are unchecked by default. Changes apply the next time you launch the game.

- **Collect detailed diagnostic logs** records additional information about game operation, target selection, aiming, and firing.
- **Collect detailed collision logs** records vehicle contacts, collision checks, and blocked movement.
- **Automatic driving** takes over vehicle movement and hull steering.
- **Automatic aiming and fire** takes over target selection, target lock, aiming, and firing.
- **Automatic consumables** uses equipped consumables as needed.
- **Start automatic control at battle start** turns on the selected automatic control features when the battle is ready.

Choose a **Test scene**, then use the regular Start button. Dedicated single-player scenes can load specific maps and Bot lineups. **Open player control panel** provides test commands such as stopping the vehicle, restoring manual control, and firing a single shot.

### Turning player automatic control on and off in battle

Before launching the game, select **Start automatic control at battle start** and at least one automatic control feature on the Testing tab. Once the battle is ready, press the **backtick key** (`` ` ``) immediately to the left of **1** on the main keyboard’s number row to turn automatic control off and return to manual control.
