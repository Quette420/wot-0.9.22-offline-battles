# v1.1.2 — Garage and fire fixes on v1.1.1

Built from `v1.1.1` on the `release/v1.1.x` maintenance branch; it does not include the v1.2.x changes.

- Customization: buying and applying a camouflage, paint, decal or effect in one step works again. #1513 sends this as a single buy-and-equip outfit command; the garage now buys only the missing units, charges for them and installs the outfit as one transaction instead of refusing with "not enough customization units".
- Crew retraining: the three schools lose role level as in retail. Same vehicle class: free course 80%, credit school 90%, gold academy 100%. Another class: 60%, 80% and 100%. The school minimum (50% / 75% / 100%) still applies. Sandbox saves keep the same rules at no cost.
- Fire damage: a fire now starts at its strongest and weakens linearly until it burns out, instead of a fixed 5% of maximum HP every second for ten seconds. Untrained crews burn for ten seconds and lose about 25% of maximum HP. Each fire draws its starting strength once (80% to 120%), so a stronger fire also lasts longer. Firefighting and other crew effects from the client shorten the fire; they do not reduce its starting damage. Bots and players use the same model, as in v1.2.x. This shape comes from community measurements; the ten-second duration and 5% starting rate are this project's own calibration, not retail data.
